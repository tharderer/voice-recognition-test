How to lead your caravan

Phone: swipe the world in the direction you want to go. Longer swipes travel farther. Tap to stop. Use INTERACT near the gold marker. RUN gives a short burst; CALL helps stragglers catch up; STAFF drives nearby wolves away. Use + / − to change the view.

Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move · E to interact · Space for staff · Q to call · Shift to run. Click the world to focus the keyboard.

Watch your caravan condition and water. Camps save your chapter on this device. If the caravan is exhausted, retry the current chapter. Pausing stops the hazards.
