# AB001 Godot Adventure — Get Thee Out of Thy Country

A Godot 4.7.2 web-game prototype for Hide the Word Hero Card AB001, based on Genesis 12:1–5 (KJV).

## Mission flow
1. Speak with Sarai.
2. Find Lot.
3. Gather three supply bundles.
4. Gather three sheep.
5. Lead the caravan through Haran's gate.
6. Follow the marked road into Canaan.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys, E / Space for action, or the large on-screen touch controls.
