# Hide the Word: Skybreak

Standalone mobile KJV Bible-memory arcade game. Open `skybreak/` from the repository's GitHub Pages site. No build, account, microphone, server, or paid API required at play time.

## Play

- Swipe left/right to steer, up to jump, down to spend a charged burst. Alternatively tap a word's lane button and use Jump/Burst. Desktop: arrows or WASD; Space jumps, H reveals, Escape pauses.
- Enter the lane with the next verse word before its gate reaches the glider. Choices remain fixed until the correct word is collected, including after a mistake.
- Coral crystals are jumpable hazards. A dashed line marks their lane. At least one lane remains hazard-free. Word gates can be collected while jumping.
- Every four consecutive words raises the score multiplier, to ×5. Five words charge a burst that clears hazards, shields the glider for four seconds, and doubles the next three correct-word awards.
- Learn shows the verse. Recall hides uncollected words. Boss uses recall and each correct word damages the fictional storm engine. Completed words remain visible in all rounds. Reveal supplies a word without ending the run.
- Choose an upgrade after Learn and Recall. Zero hearts offers a rescue at the current word. All menus scroll in either orientation; changing tabs pauses gameplay.
- Complete eight selectable missions. Local medals and best scores are saved on this browser/device, not synced. Gold requires no errors or hints. Shorter starting modes are challenges, not proof of memorization.

Scripture text is KJV. The sky world, storm machine, and power-ups are fictional game mechanics, not biblical events. The background is an original generated game asset. CSS and JavaScript are separate from the HTML.
